# Product brief

## Purpose

This document defines how FitWell's product brief must be maintained. The brief is the stable orientation layer for future PRDs and should explain product intent without claiming implementation detail.

## Required content

The maintained brief must contain:

1. **Vision:** the durable change FitWell seeks to create for its users.
2. **Intended users:** primary and secondary audiences described by needs, not demographics alone.
3. **Problems:** the fitness-planning or tracking problems the product chooses to solve.
4. **Primary journeys:** outcome-level journeys such as beginning, recording, reviewing, or managing fitness activity.
5. **Product principles:** decision filters for usability, privacy, trust, and scope.
6. **Boundaries:** capabilities inside the product and explicit non-goals.
7. **Success signals:** observable product outcomes without inventing unsupported metrics.

## Writing rules

- Use user and administrator language, not technology names.
- Describe durable intent, not current screen layouts.
- Do not list unverified capabilities; those belong in the feature catalog after verification.
- Do not turn priorities into requirements.
- Link domain PRDs when a principle has a binding expression.

## Responsibilities and review

The project owner approves changes. Review the brief when product strategy, target users, or scope boundaries change. Internal refactors and routine feature delivery do not require an update unless they change the product's purpose.

## Example distinction

Informational brief statement: “FitWell should make workout recording understandable during an active session.”

Binding follow-up: a Workout Engine PRD defines the exact observable outcomes and edge cases.
